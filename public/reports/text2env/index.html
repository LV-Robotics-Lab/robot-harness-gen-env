Taxonomy and Command Boundary

selection2env retrieves; generation2env creates.

TermProject definition
text2taskGenerate task definitions, constraints, bindings, and success verifiers from language without necessarily changing the scene.
text2sceneGenerate or assemble the spatial scene layout from language, including rooms, support surfaces, and object placement.
text2assetGenerate or reconstruct geometry, articulation, textures, materials, or physics metadata for assets.
selection2envP0 route that retrieves and places existing execution-eligible assets, then emits reusable scene and task specs without inventing new geometry.
generation2envP1/P2 route that creates missing assets or scenes and must pass geometry, scale, collision, support, material, import, and verifier gates before use.
sim_in_loop_repairRun static and embodied checks, diagnose failures, patch scene/task/program specifications, and re-execute regression cases.
domain_randomization_data_generationGenerate controlled placement, appearance, language, embodiment, and trajectory variation for collection and training.
policy_eval_data_hookConnect scene/task artifacts to collection, training, evaluation, diagnosis, promotion, and failure-to-data decisions.
